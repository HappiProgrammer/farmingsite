# Product Overview

## What this is
A premium, documentary-style website for the AIVDP/SOWEDA oil-palm farmer
development project. It exists to prove three things at once:
1. The project is real and has measurable impact.
2. Real, named farmers are benefiting from it.
3. Participating farmers get a lasting showcase where buyers, agricultural
   organizations, and other stakeholders can discover their nurseries and
   available seedlings.

## Audiences
- Funders, partners, and AIVDP/SOWEDA stakeholders evaluating the project
- Participating farmers, who each get a shareable profile page
- Seedling buyers and agricultural organizations looking for nursery stock
- General public / press

## Creative concept
"From Support to Growth" — a scroll-driven narrative:
Support → Farmer → Nursery → Seedlings → Growth → Impact → Opportunity.
The site should feel like a documentary and impact report first, a
directory second — never a generic NGO template, a stock-photo agri site,
or an e-commerce store.

## Explicit non-goals (v1)
- No payments, checkout, or purchasing flow — contact/WhatsApp only
- No fabricated statistics, quotes, names, dates, or partnerships — use
  clearly bracketed placeholders (e.g. `[NUMBER OF FARMERS]`) for anything
  not yet supplied
- No exposing exact personal addresses — locations are community/area level
